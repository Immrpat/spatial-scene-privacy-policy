01 / ABOUT

Who this policy covers

Namib develops and publishes Spatial Scene for Android (package name com.namib.spatialapp). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Namib.

Spatial Scene turns 2D photographs into interactive 3D parallax scenes, live wallpapers, and short 3D sweep videos using an on-device neural network and OpenGL ES 3.0 shaders.

No user account is required. We do not operate servers that receive your photos, depth maps, sensor data, or usage telemetry, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

02 / LOCAL PROCESSING

Photos and on-device AI depth

When you tap Gallery, Spatial Scene opens Android’s system Photo Picker. Android provides temporary read access strictly to the single image you choose; the app cannot browse or scan the rest of your photo library.

All image processing happens locally on your phone or tablet:

  • The selected photo is decoded at full resolution (respecting EXIF orientation) in device memory.
  • A resized copy is passed locally to the bundled Depth Anything V2 Small (FP16) model via ONNX Runtime Mobile to estimate 16-bit depth and edge inpainting textures.
  • Your original photo file is never modified, and neither the photo nor its depth map is transmitted off your device.

If you select an image supplied by a cloud-backed Android media provider inside the system Photo Picker, Android or that provider handles any download needed to hand the file to your device under that provider’s own privacy policy.

03 / DEVICE SENSORS

Motion sensors and gyroscope tilt

To produce real-time 3D tilt parallax as you move your phone, Spatial Scene reads your device’s motion sensors (TYPE_ROTATION_VECTOR or TYPE_GAME_ROTATION_VECTOR) inside the viewer and the Live Wallpaper service.

Motion sensor readings are processed strictly in memory for live rendering and smoothing. They are never saved to disk, recorded, or transmitted anywhere.

04 / SAVING & SHARING

Live wallpaper, MP4 export, and sharing

Spatial Scene saves files only when you explicitly choose to set a Live Wallpaper or export a video:

  • Live Wallpaper: Tapping Wallpaper saves the active scene’s color image, edge-inpainted background texture, packed 16-bit depth texture, and your depth-intensity/focal-plane settings inside the app’s private storage (files/spatial_wallpaper/ and private SharedPreferences) so Android’s Live Wallpaper service can render the scene on your Home and Lock screens.
  • Export MP4: Tapping Export MP4 renders a 3-second (60 fps, H.264) orbital camera sweep into temporary private cache, saves the finished MP4 to your device Gallery under Movies/SpatialScene via Android MediaStore, and removes the temporary cache copy.
  • Share: Tapping Share after an export opens Android’s system share sheet so you can send the MP4 to an app or destination you select. Once shared, that destination handles the video under its own privacy policy.

05 / PERMISSIONS & LIBRARIES

Permissions and third-party software

Spatial Scene is built to operate completely offline. It does not request permissions for INTERNET, CAMERA, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, contacts, microphone, or broad storage/photo-library scanning (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).

The app bundles Microsoft ONNX Runtime Mobile and the Depth Anything V2 Small (FP16) model weights inside the app package solely to run local depth inference on your device’s CPU or hardware accelerator. To initialize the runtime, the model file is copied once into the app’s private cache directory.

The app contains no third-party advertising networks, social SDKs, analytics services, or remote crash reporters.

06 / RETENTION & DELETION

How long local files stay and how to delete them

We do not hold your photos or scenes on any server. Local files remain under your control on your device:

Active Live Wallpaper scene and preferences
Saved in private app storage until you replace the scene with another photo, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall Spatial Scene.
Cached AI model file
Stored in private app cache to speed up subsequent launches. Removed when you clear app cache in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
Exported MP4 videos
Saved to Movies/SpatialScene in your device’s media library. Exported videos remain in your Gallery after uninstalling the app until you delete them yourself.
Android system backup
The app manifest permits standard Android device backup (allowBackup). You can manage or disable cloud device backups in your Android system settings.

07 / SECURITY

How local data is protected

Scene textures and preferences used by the Live Wallpaper are stored in Android’s private app sandbox, isolated from other apps on your device. Because Spatial Scene never transmits your photos or depth data over a network, your imagery is not exposed in transit to external servers.

Protection of files stored on your phone or exported to your Gallery depends on your device’s screen lock, operating system encryption, and the sharing apps you choose to use.

08 / CHILDREN
